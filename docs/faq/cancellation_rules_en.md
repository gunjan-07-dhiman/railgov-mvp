# Cancellation Rules & Clerkage Charges (Demo Content)

*Disclaimer: Demonstration reference rules for academic evaluation. Not official railway terms.*

### Cancellation Deadlines & Charges
1. **RAC and Waitlisted Tickets**:
   - If an RAC or Waitlisted ticket is cancelled up to 30 minutes before the scheduled departure of the train, a flat clerkage charge of ₹60 per passenger is deducted, and the balance fare is refunded.
   - No clerkage refund is allowed if cancelled less than 30 minutes before departure.
2. **Train Cancellation by Railways**:
   - If a train is cancelled by Indian Railways due to unforeseen operational, track, or weather reasons, a 100% full refund of the fare is automatically credited without any clerkage deduction.
3. **Filing TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt)**:
   - For passengers who could not travel due to late train running (over 3 hours late) or AC failure, an online TDR must be filed before train departure to claim a refund.
