# Waitlist (WL) vs. RAC Meaning & Travel Eligibility (Demo Content)

### Waitlist and RAC Explained
1. **RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)**:
   - Passengers holding RAC tickets are permitted to board the train and travel.
   - You are assigned a shared sitting seat (normally Side Lower, shared between two RAC ticket holders).
   - If any confirmed passenger cancels or fails to show up, RAC passengers are upgraded to full sleeping berths in order of status.
2. **Waiting List (WL)**:
   - A fully waitlisted ticket (e-ticket) does not give any right to board reserved coaches.
   - If an online e-ticket remains in WL status after chart preparation, the system cancels it automatically and refunds the fare.
   - Travelling on a fully waitlisted e-ticket in reserved coaches is treated as travelling without ticket and attracts penalty under railway rules.
   - If a group e-ticket has both confirmed and WL passengers, waitlisted passengers may travel only if RAC is allocated during charting.
