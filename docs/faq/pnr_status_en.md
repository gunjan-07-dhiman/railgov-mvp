# PNR Status Meanings & Codes (Demo Content)

*Disclaimer: Demonstration reference guide for YatraGov AI. Not official policy.*

### Understanding PNR Status Codes
1. **CNF (Confirmed)**: A complete, reserved seat or berth has been assigned to your ticket. Specific coach and berth numbers appear upon final reservation chart preparation.
2. **RAC (Reservation Against Cancellation)**: You have a confirmed right to board the train and travel, with a shared sitting seat (side lower). If confirmed passengers cancel, RAC tickets are upgraded to full berths.
3. **WL (Waitlist)**: Your booking is in the waiting list queue. You have no seat allotted. If the ticket remains in WL after chart preparation, an online ticket is automatically cancelled and refunded; passengers holding WL tickets cannot board reserved coaches.
4. **CAN (Cancelled)**: The booking has been cancelled by the passenger or the system.
5. **Chart Prepared**: Reservation charts are generally prepared 4 hours prior to train departure (or the previous night for early morning departures).
