# How to Use YatraGov AI Platform (Demo Content)

*Disclaimer: Demonstration guidance for YatraGov AI. Not official policy.*

### Platform Features and Usage Guide
1. **Ticket Confirmation Predictor**:
   - Calculates calibrated historical confirmation probability for waitlisted and RAC tickets.
   - Provides TreeSHAP log-odds explanations showing which journey factors (such as lead days, route popularity, and travel class) influence confirmation odds.
2. **Train Delay & Platform Crowd Forecast**:
   - Estimates expected delay range in minutes using historical route telemetry and environmental conditions.
   - Predicts station crowd density (Low, Moderate, High) and provides proactive arrival advice.
3. **Dynamic Fare & Demand Surge Advisor**:
   - Evaluates estimated standard fare ranges and scans a 60-day booking horizon to find the optimal day to purchase tickets.
   - Performs price anomaly checks on quoted fares.
4. **Booking Fraud Shield**:
   - Analyzes interaction velocity and device attributes to detect automated high-frequency bot traffic.
5. **Support Chatbot**:
   - Grounded multilingual travel guide answering questions from official FAQ documentation and looking up 10-digit PNR statuses.
