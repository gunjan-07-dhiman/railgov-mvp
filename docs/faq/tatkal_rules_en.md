# Tatkal Booking Rules & Timings (Demo Content)

*Disclaimer: Demonstration reference guide for YatraGov AI. Not official policy.*

### Tatkal Quota Rules
1. **Opening Timings**:
   - **AC Classes (2A, 3A, 3E, CC)**: Booking opens at **10:00 AM IST**, exactly one day prior to the train departure date from the originating station.
   - **Non-AC Classes (Sleeper, Second Sitting)**: Booking opens at **11:00 AM IST**, exactly one day prior to departure.
2. **Refund Policy on Tatkal Tickets**:
   - **No refund** of fare is granted on cancellation of a confirmed Tatkal ticket.
   - For waitlisted Tatkal tickets, cancellation follows standard clerkage deduction rules if cancelled prior to chart preparation.
   - Full refund is granted only if the train is cancelled or delayed by more than 3 hours.
3. **Identification**: Valid government-issued photo identity proof is required during journey.
