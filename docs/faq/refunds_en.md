### Online Ticket Cancellation Refunds
1. **Confirmed Tickets**: If a confirmed ticket is cancelled more than 48 hours before the scheduled departure of the train, flat cancellation charges are deducted per passenger based on class (AC First: ₹240, AC 2 Tier: ₹200, AC 3 Tier: ₹180, Sleeper: ₹120, Second Class: ₹60).
2. **Cancellation Timing**:
   - Between 48 hours and 12 hours before departure: 25% of the fare subject to minimum flat charges.
   - Between 12 hours and 4 hours before departure (up to chart preparation): 50% of the fare subject to minimum flat charges.
   - Less than 4 hours before departure or after chart preparation: No refund is admissible on confirmed tickets.
3. **Refund Processing**: Electronic ticket refunds are processed automatically back to the original booking payment gateway within 3 to 7 working days.
